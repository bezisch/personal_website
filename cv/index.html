Profile

Researcher in the political economy of the environment. Postdoctoral fellow at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research and incoming Walter Benjamin Scholar at the European University Institute. PhD in Economics from Scuola Superiore Sant’Anna, Pisa, joint with the Université Côte d’Azur, Nice.

Works on climate obstruction, just transitions, environmental inequality, and the geography of large-scale industries, including AI data centres. Combines causal econometrics with text-as-data methods and qualitative research.

Methods & skills

  • Causal econometrics: difference-in-differences, quantile regression, panel models, spatial analysis
  • Text-as-data: structural and dynamic topic models, named entity recognition, sentiment analysis, supervised stance classification
  • Qualitative research: elite interviews, document coding, process tracing, collaborative research with environmental justice organisations
  • Research design: survey experiments, pre-registration, mixed-methods triangulation
  • Languages: works in English, German, Italian, and Spanish
