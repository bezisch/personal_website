08 About

About

Researcher in the political economy of the environment, writer, and occasional triathlete.

I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK), Germany, where I work in the Climate Economics and Policy Department. I am an incoming Walter Benjamin Scholar at the Chair of Computational Social Science at the European University Institute in Florence.

Until June 2025, I was a Fulbright Schumann scholar at the Tishman Environment and Design Center at The New School in New York, and I have an ongoing affiliation as a visiting researcher. I co-lead the CSSN project Peddling false solutions: corporate carbon management and climate delay strategies with Brown University, jointly with Thomas Klug. In spring 2024, I visited the Universidad de los Andes in Bogotá, Colombia, as part of a cross-continental research grant by the Environment for Development Initiative.

My research focuses on the political economy of the environment. I study obstacles to climate action, just transition narratives, and the geography of large-scale industries, including AI data centres. To do so, I apply causal econometric methods and cutting-edge text-as-data methods within computational social science.

Besides my academic life, I have worked as a consultant for private and public entities. I also write for non-academic outlets, for instance Jacobin Italia (in Italian), Social Europe (in English), Süddeutsche Zeitung (in German), and Viento Sur (in Spanish). And I spend a lot of time training for triathlons.

I have a PhD in Economics from the Scuola Superiore Sant’Anna, Pisa, Italy, a joint programme with the Université Côte d’Azur, Nice, France (2018–2022). From May to August 2022, I was a visiting scholar at the Political Economy Research Institute (PERI) at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, where I worked with Michael Ash and James Boyce. Moreover, I was an associated researcher at the Bureau of Theoretical and Applied Economics (BETA), Strasbourg, France, and a fellow at the Bocconi Lab in European Studies (BLES), Milan, Italy.

I am part of the CSSN network on climate obstruction and of the working group “Crisis and Socio-Ecological Transformation” by the Rosa Luxemburg Foundation.
